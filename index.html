About AfroCares

We Are More Than a Medical Coordinator — We Are Your Guardian

AfroCares was founded by healthcare professionals and social workers who witnessed the exploitation of African and international patients seeking medical treatment in India. Patients were losing thousands of dollars to fraudulent agents, being sent to unaccredited clinics, and left without support in a foreign land.

As a registered Non-Governmental Organisation (NGO) in India, we bridge the gap between international patients and India's world-class hospitals — completely free of cost. We receive no commission from hospitals. Our funding comes from donations and grants, ensuring our advice is always in your best interest.

Our Mission & Values

Why We Exist

AfroCares stands on three unshakeable pillars that guide every decision we make.

Protection

We shield patients from fraudulent medical agents, overpriced packages, and unaccredited facilities that prey on vulnerable patients seeking care abroad.

Quality Care

We connect patients only to NABH-accredited and JCI-certified hospitals with proven track records in the specific treatments patients need.

Access for All

Medical tourism should not be a luxury. We advocate for affordable treatment options and help patients find the right care regardless of their budget.

⚠️ Know the Signs of Medical Tourism Scams in India

Agents demanding large upfront payments before you arrive
Unverifiable hospital accreditation or credentials
No written treatment plans or cost estimates
Pressure to decide quickly or "lose the spot"
No physical address or India-registered contact number
Promising unrealistic cure rates for serious conditions

If you've experienced any of the above — contact AfroCares immediately before making any payments.

Our Free Services for International Patients

From your first inquiry to your safe return home — AfroCares supports you at every stage, at zero cost.

Hospital Matching

We match you to the best-suited, accredited hospital for your specific medical condition, budget, and location preference within India.

Second Medical Opinion

Unsure about a diagnosis? We arrange free virtual consultations with leading Indian specialists to give you a verified second opinion.

Medical Visa Guidance

We guide you through the Indian medical visa process, documentation requirements, and invitation letter procedures — step by step.

Treatment Cost Estimates

Receive transparent, written cost estimates from multiple hospitals so you can compare and make an informed, pressure-free decision.

Arrival & Logistics Support

Airport pickup coordination, accommodation guidance near your hospital, and local SIM/connectivity help on arrival in India.

Language & Interpretation

Our multilingual team and network of interpreters help bridge language barriers between you and your medical team throughout treatment.

Scam Investigation & Help

If you've been defrauded by an agent or fake hospital, we help you report it to Indian authorities and guide you on next steps.

Post-Treatment Follow-Up

We stay with you after treatment — coordinating follow-up care, medical records, and connecting you with doctors for teleconsultations back home.

Community & Support Groups

Join our WhatsApp and online communities of verified patients, ask questions, share experiences, and get peer support from people who've been through it.

Why Choose India

03

Dramatic Cost Savings

Heart surgery costing \$100,000 in the US may cost \$6,000–\$10,000 in India — with equal or better outcomes.

04

No Long Waiting Lists

The Process

How AfroCares Works — Step by Step

Getting started is simple. We handle the complexity so you can focus on your health.

1

Contact Us

Reach out via WhatsApp, email, or our contact form. Share basic information about your condition, medical reports, and what kind of treatment you're seeking.

2

Free Case Assessment

Our medical coordination team reviews your case. We may consult with doctors in our network to understand the best specialties and hospitals for your condition.

3

Hospital Shortlist & Cost Estimate

We share 2–3 verified hospital options with treatment plans and transparent cost estimates. No pressure — you choose what feels right for you.

4

Visa & Travel Guidance

We provide a medical visa invitation letter and walk you through the Indian medical visa application process for your country.

5

Arrive in India — We're Here

Our team coordinates your airport reception, hospital admission, and local needs. You are never alone on the ground in India.

6

Treatment & Support

Receive your treatment with our team available 24/7 to assist with communication, hospital liaison, accommodation, and any emergencies.

7

Return Home & Follow-Up

We help you get discharge documents, medication lists, and connect you with teleconsultation options for continued care in your home country.
